01 Atelier

An interior practice with a future bias.

{{ theme.brand }} is a spatial atelier working between Dubai, Singapore, and Oslo. We design rooms for people who expect the next decade to feel considered, not louder.

The studio began as a light workshop and grew into full environments: private houses, hotels, baths, and the rooms where companies think. We still prototype finishes before we promise them.

Clients come for a look and stay for the behavior of the room. Temperature, hush, the path of a person at night, the way a material ages after the photographs are done.
